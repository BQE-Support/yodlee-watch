      How this works
      • Data. A sync PC at BQE signs in to Yodlee Orbitus over the BQE VPN, reads every service request assigned to BQE and its worklog, and joins it to Zendesk through the Yodlee Number field (or the SR number in the ticket text when the field is empty). Open Zendesk tickets tagged as escalated to Yodlee are included even without an SR.
      • Updates. Automatically every 12 hours. The Refresh button asks the sync PC for a new pull; it picks the request up within about 5 minutes. Limits: one pull every 15 minutes across everyone, and 4 manual refreshes per person per day. The PC has to be on and on the VPN.
      • Status timing. Orbitus keeps no status history, so "since" times are when a pull first saw the status. For SRs that already had their status before Yodlee Watch started, the SR's last-updated time is used.
      • Alerts. Mismatch: the SR is still Open/Pending in Orbitus but the Zendesk ticket is solved or closed (close the SR or reopen the ticket). Yodlee resolved: Orbitus says Resolved while the ticket is still open (confirm with the customer). Waiting on BQE: Orbitus status Pending. Yodlee replied: Yodlee's latest public reply is newer than the ticket's last update. Gone quiet: no public reply from Yodlee in 7+ days (14+ is red). Business-day timers skip weekends.
      • Done checkboxes are saved in your browser only; they don't change anything in Orbitus or Zendesk, and an alert comes back if its facts change.
      • Orbitus links open the Service requests list (Orbitus has no per-request link). Search the SR number there.
